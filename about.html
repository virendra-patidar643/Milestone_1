How we treat you

Every patient follows the same simple 4 steps.

1. Assessment

We begin with a detailed talk about your pain, your daily routine and your medical history. Then we check your movement, posture and strength to find the real cause, not just the spot that hurts.

2. Treatment plan

Based on the assessment, we set clear recovery goals with you. You get a simple plan that explains the treatment, the exercises and how many sessions you may need.

3. Therapy sessions

Each session is one-to-one with your therapist. We use hands-on therapy and guided exercises, and we explain every step so you know what is being done and why.

4. Progress review

We check your recovery at regular intervals and change the plan when needed. You also get simple home exercises so your progress continues between visits.

Our values

What we promise to every patient who walks in.

Patient first

Your comfort, your goals and your daily routine come before everything else. We listen fully before we suggest any treatment.

Honest advice

We tell you exactly what you need. If fewer sessions are enough, we say so, and we never push extra sessions or treatments.

Step-by-step recovery

Healing takes time, so we move in small, safe steps. Each step rebuilds your strength and your confidence to return to normal life.

Clear pricing

You know the cost of each session before you begin, so there are no surprises later.
